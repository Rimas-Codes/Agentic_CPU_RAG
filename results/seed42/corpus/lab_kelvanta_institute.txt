The Kelvanta Institute was founded in 1988 by Freya Mbeki. It is located in the city of Thornby. The team publishes its results in open journals and shares its tools with partner groups.

Today the Kelvanta Institute employs 105 researchers. Its flagship effort is Project Harbinger, which receives most of the institute's attention. Visitors are welcome to attend the public seminars held on the first Friday of every month.

The Mirnavel Institute was founded in 1973 by Dmitri Delacroix. It is located in the city of Kessoby. Visitors are welcome to attend the public seminars held on the first Friday of every month.

Today the Mirnavel Institute employs 264 researchers. Its flagship effort is Project Ember, which receives most of the institute's attention. Funding discussions are handled by an independent board that meets every quarter.

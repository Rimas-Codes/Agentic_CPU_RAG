The Ulmeris Institute was founded in 1958 by Yara Abernathy. It is located in the city of Embervale. Annual reports are written in plain language so that non-specialists can follow the work.

Today the Ulmeris Institute employs 292 researchers. Its flagship effort is Project Vesper, which receives most of the institute's attention. Visitors are welcome to attend the public seminars held on the first Friday of every month.

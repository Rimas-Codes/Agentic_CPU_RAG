The Veldris Institute was founded in 1999 by Emeka Moreau. It is located in the city of Altamere. Visitors are welcome to attend the public seminars held on the first Friday of every month.

Today the Veldris Institute employs 696 researchers. Its flagship effort is Project Aurora, which receives most of the institute's attention. Annual reports are written in plain language so that non-specialists can follow the work.
